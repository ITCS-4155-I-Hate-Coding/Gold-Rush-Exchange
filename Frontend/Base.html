About

A social prediction exchange built for Niner Nation

Gold Rush Exchange is a social sports-prediction site for UNC Charlotte students. Pick winners in college football and basketball, back your picks with reputation points, and see who really knows the game. There's no real money involved, only bragging rights.

Free to join @charlotte.edu accounts only No real money

How reputation works

  1. Start with 1,000 rep. Everyone gets the same bankroll.
  2. Wager on a pick. Choose a side before the game starts and stake any amount you have. One pick per game.
  3. Right? You get your wager back twice: stake 100, get 200.
  4. Wrong? The wager is gone.
  5. Tie or cancelled game? Your wager is refunded.
  6. Ran out? Below 50 rep with no open picks, you're topped back up to 50 (once a day).

Example

Starting balance1,000
Wager 100 on Charlotte−100
Charlotte wins: wager back ×2+200
New balance1,100

If Charlotte had lost, the balance would stay at 900.

Questions

Is any real money involved?

No. Reputation points can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

Who can join?

Anyone with a @charlotte.edu email address.

Where do the games and scores come from?

ESPN's public scoreboard for FBS college football and men's college basketball. The site checks for new games and final scores every 15 minutes and settles picks automatically.

When do picks close?

When the game is scheduled to start.

Who can message me?

Only friends: people you follow who also follow you back. If either of you unfollows, the old messages stay but no new ones can be sent.

How is my account protected?

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, never as plain text, and too many wrong passwords locks the login for 15 minutes.
