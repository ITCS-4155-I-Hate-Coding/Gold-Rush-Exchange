# Gold-Rush-Exchange998

## Project Overview

Our project is a social platform designed for UNCC students and sports fans to interact with friends through sports predictions and community discussions.

Users can make predictions on upcoming sporting events using **reputation points instead of real money**, discuss games with other users, create posts, follow friends, and compete on leaderboards. Users can build their reputation through successful predictions and community participation.

The goal of the platform is to combine sports predictions with social interaction in one place, giving students a way to compete with friends and engage with their sports communities.

---

## Features

### 🏆 Predictions

* Make predictions on upcoming games and sporting events.
* Wager reputation points on predictions.
* View prediction results after an event is completed.
* Gain or lose reputation points based on prediction results.
* Track prediction accuracy and winning streaks.

### 💬 Social Feed

* Create posts about games, teams, and predictions.
* Like posts and comments.
* Comment and reply to other users.
* Follow friends and view their activity.
* Discover trending predictions and discussions.

### ⭐ Reputation Points

* Earn reputation through successful predictions and community activity.
* Gain points from posting, commenting, and receiving likes.
* Lose points when predictions are incorrect.
* Use reputation points to compare performance with other users.

### 👥 Friends & Following

* Follow friends and other users.
* View friends' predictions and activity.
* Compare prediction records and reputation scores.
* See content from users you follow.

### 📊 Leaderboards

* Global reputation leaderboard.
* Friend-based leaderboards.
* Weekly and monthly rankings.
* Prediction accuracy statistics.
* Winning streak statistics.
* Total prediction statistics.

### 📜 Prediction History

* View previous predictions.
* Track wins and losses.
* Calculate prediction accuracy.
* View reputation gained or lost.
* Track performance over time.

### 🏟️ Game & Team Pages

* View upcoming games.
* View available predictions.
* Display team and game information.
* View community predictions.
* Discuss games through posts and comments.

### 🔥 Trending Content

* Highlight popular predictions and discussions.
* Rank content based on likes, comments, and activity.
* Discover popular content within the community.

### 👤 User Profiles

* Display reputation score.
* Display leaderboard ranking.
* Show prediction accuracy and win/loss record.
* Display recent predictions and posts.
* Display achievements and winning streaks.

### 🏅 Achievements

Users can earn achievements for reaching milestones such as:

* 10 Prediction Streak

* Community participation milestones

### 🔔 Notifications

* Notifications for post likes and comments.

### 🚨 Content Reporting & Moderation

* Report inappropriate posts and comments.

## Reputation System

The platform uses a reputation-point system instead of real money.

Users can gain or lose reputation points based on their activity and prediction performance.

| Activity             | Reputation |
| -------------------- | ---------- |
| Correct prediction   | + Points   |
| Incorrect prediction | - Points   |
| Create a post        | + Points   |
| Comment              | + Points   |
| Receive likes        | + Points   |
| Achievements         | + Points   |

The exact point values may be adjusted during development and testing.

**No real money is used for predictions.**
