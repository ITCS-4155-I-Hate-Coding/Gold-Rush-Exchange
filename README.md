# Gold-Rush-Exchangebackend2

## Overview

**Gold-Rush-Exchange** is a social sports prediction platform designed for college students and sports fans.

Users can predict the outcomes of sporting events using **virtual reputation points**, interact with other users, follow friends, discuss games, and compete on leaderboards.

The platform does **not use real money**. Reputation points are used to track prediction performance and participation within the community.

---

# How to Use Gold-Rush-Exchange

## 1. Create an Account

When a user first visits Gold-Rush-Exchange, they can create an account.

Users provide:

* Username
* Charlotte email address
* Password

For the current version, users must use a university email ending in:

```text
@charlotte.edu
```

After creating an account, the user can log in and access the rest of the platform.

---

## 2. View the Home Page

After logging in, users are taken to the main page.

The home page allows users to see:

* Upcoming sporting events
* Popular predictions
* Trending posts
* Recent activity from people they follow
* Leaderboard information

This gives users a quick overview of what is happening on the platform.

---

## 3. Find a Game

Users can browse upcoming games and sporting events.

Selecting a game takes the user to its game page, where they can see information such as:

* Teams playing
* Game date and time
* Available predictions
* Community predictions
* Related discussions

---

## 4. Make a Prediction

Once a user finds a game, they can make a prediction.

For example, a user could select:

```text
Charlotte vs. Duke

Prediction:
Charlotte will win

Reputation wager:
100 points
```

The user submits the prediction using their reputation points.

No real money is involved.

After the game is completed, the prediction is marked as either correct or incorrect.

* Correct prediction → User gains reputation points
* Incorrect prediction → User loses reputation points

Users can view their prediction results in their prediction history.

---

## 5. View Prediction History

Users can open their prediction history to see how they have performed over time.

The history can show:

* Previous predictions
* Wins
* Losses
* Prediction accuracy
* Reputation gained
* Reputation lost
* Winning streaks

This allows users to track how successful they have been with their predictions.

---

## 6. Create a Post

Users can participate in the social side of the platform by creating posts.

For example:

```text
I think Charlotte is going to win tonight.
Their defense has looked really good recently.
```

Posts can be about:

* Games
* Teams
* Predictions
* Sports news
* Discussions with other users

---

## 7. Interact With Other Users

Users can interact with posts and other members of the community.

They can:

* Like posts
* Comment on posts
* Reply to comments
* Follow other users
* View friends' activity
* Discuss predictions

Users can also receive reputation for participating in the community.

---

## 8. Follow Friends

Users can follow friends and other users.

After following someone, their activity can appear in the user's feed.

For example, users can see when someone they follow:

* Makes a prediction
* Creates a post
* Participates in a discussion
* Reaches an achievement

Users can also compare their prediction performance with their friends.

---

## 9. Check the Leaderboards

Users can view leaderboards to compare their reputation and prediction performance with other users.

The platform supports different types of rankings, including:

### Global Leaderboard

Shows users with the highest reputation on the platform.

### Friends Leaderboard

Allows users to compare their reputation and statistics with people they follow.

### Weekly and Monthly Leaderboards

Allows users to see recent performance over different time periods.

Statistics can include:

* Reputation
* Prediction accuracy
* Total predictions
* Winning streaks
* Wins and losses

---

## 10. Earn Achievements

Users can earn achievements by reaching different milestones.

Examples include:

* **10 Prediction Streak**
* **100 Predictions**
* **Top 10 Weekly**
* Community participation milestones

Achievements give users additional goals to work toward while using the platform.

---

## 11. Check Notifications

Users receive notifications when something important happens to their account.

Notifications can include:

* Someone liking their post
* Someone commenting on their post
* A friend making a prediction
* A prediction being completed
* A change in leaderboard position

---

## 12. Report or Block Content

Users can report posts or comments that violate the community rules.

Users can also:

* Block other users
* Mute other users
* Report inappropriate content

Moderators can review reported content and take appropriate action.

---

# Reputation System

Gold-Rush-Exchange uses a virtual reputation system instead of real money.

Users can gain or lose reputation based on their activity.

| Activity             | Reputation |
| -------------------- | ---------: |
| Correct prediction   |   + Points |
| Incorrect prediction |   - Points |
| Create a post        |   + Points |
| Comment              |   + Points |
| Receive likes        |   + Points |
| Achievement          |   + Points |

The exact values can be changed during development and testing.

**No real money is used for predictions.**
